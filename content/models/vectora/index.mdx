# Vectora model family

Vectora documents educational language-model implementations and their
versioned technical records. Each release has its own page, evidence scope,
diagrams, and reproducibility notes.

## Available versions

- [Vectora v1.2](/models/vectora/v1.2) — **23M Dataset Experiment (Latest Release)**.
  An experimental character-level GPT-style Transformer trained from scratch across
  the complete 82,215,804-character corpus (~23.4M estimated GPT-2 tokens) over 50,000
  iterations, achieving a final validation loss of 1.4082 on an expanded 333-character vocabulary.

- [Vectora v1.1](/models/vectora/v1.1) — An experimental character-level
  GPT-style Transformer trained from scratch on a 500,000-character subset of Shakespeare text
  over 10,000 iterations with recorded loss metrics and generated text samples.

- [Vectora v1](/models/vectora/v1) — A documented educational decoder-only
  Transformer using GPT-2 tokenization. The supplied notebook describes a
  randomly initialized model and demonstrates a forward pass and greedy
  generation. It serves as an architectural baseline.
